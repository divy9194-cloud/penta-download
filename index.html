What is Penta AI?

Penta AI is a voice assistant app for Android made by Divyanshu Kumar. You can speak to Penta like a friend, and it answers in Hindi, English or Hinglish.

Penta AI features

  • AI voice chat with a friendly character, with a boy or girl voice
  • Open apps, search, call, message, set alarms and timers by voice
  • Camera assistant: show something and ask about it
  • Share your screen and ask what to do next
  • Create and edit photos with AI
  • A floating Penta box that stays over other apps
