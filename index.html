PENTA

Your friendly AI voice assistant for Android

⬇ Download APK
Version 1.0 · Android 7.0 or newer · about 52 MB

What Penta can do

  • Talk to you by voice and reply in Hindi, English or Hinglish
  • Open apps, search, call, message, set alarms and timers
  • Camera assistant: show something and ask about it
  • Share your screen and ask what to do next
  • Create and edit photos with AI
  • A floating Penta box that stays over other apps

How to install

  1. Tap Download APK and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser.
  3. If Play Protect warns, tap More details → Install anyway.
  4. Open Penta, sign up and allow the permissions you need.
